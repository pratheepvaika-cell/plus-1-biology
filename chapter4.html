Unit I: Diversity in the Living World

Chapter 4: Animal Kingdom

Key Concepts:

  • Non-Chordates: Phyla ranging from Porifera to Hemichordata (Sponges, Coelenterates, Flatworms, Roundworms, Annelids, Arthropods, Molluscs, Echinoderms)[cite: 5].
  • Chordates: Animals characterized by the presence of a notochord, classified into Cyclostomata, Fishes, Amphibians, Reptiles, Aves, and Mammals[cite: 5].
